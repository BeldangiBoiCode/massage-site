Your first visit

Arrive about 10 minutes early. Everything else, we'll walk through together.

  1. Check in

    A short chat about where it hurts, past injuries and the pressure you like.

  2. Settle in

    Undress to your comfort level and get under the sheets in a warm room.

  3. The massage

    Speak up anytime: more pressure, less, or skip an area entirely.

  4. Take it home

    Water, a minute to sit up slowly, and a stretch or two to try this week.
